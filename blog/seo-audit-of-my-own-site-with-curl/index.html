A 15-minute SEO audit of my own site with curl

26 October 2026 · 2 min read

I asked how my site was doing in search. It turned out curl and my own analytics API answered most of it.

The files that should exist

for p in robots.txt sitemap.xml llms.txt favicon.ico og-image.png; do
  printf "%s -> " $p
  curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code} %{content_type}\n' https://nabilmh.com/$p
done

og-image.png and favicon.ico were both 404. The page's Open Graph tags pointed at an image that did not exist, so shared links had no preview. The sitemap listed one URL, with a last-modified date from April.

The page itself

Title, description, canonical, Open Graph, Twitter card and JSON-LD were all there. One H1, nine H2s, about 936 words, 87 KB of HTML served with Brotli in 110 ms. Performance was not the problem.

The www record

www.nabilmh.com returned an empty response. The DNS record pointed at a parking IP, proxied through Cloudflare, and the apex domain pointed at Vercel. Nobody had noticed because nobody types the www.

What the traffic says

My self-hosted analytics (Piqo, with a stats API) says 8 visitors and 13 pageviews in the last 7 days. 75% were direct, and the rest came from LinkedIn, Google Scholar and one small site. Search Console reported 0 impressions. The site is not ranking for anything beyond my own name, which is what you would expect from a single page.

My mistake

My first pass said the homepage had no internal links. It does. I had searched for hrefs starting with /, and the site uses relative links like href="3d-portfolio.html". Run grep -o 'href="[^"]*"' before concluding anything about links.

What I fixed

I generated the missing image and icons, added the pages to the sitemap, added an llms.txt, and republished my old Medium and Substack posts under /blog/. The www record still needs a domain added in Vercel. I will check the numbers again in 30 days.

Disclosure: Claude Code did most of the typing here. I checked the results by looking at them, and the mistakes below are real ones it made along the way.
